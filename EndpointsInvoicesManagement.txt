GET /api/invoices - List all invoices with filtering and pagination
POST /api/invoices - Create a new invoice
GET /api/invoices/stats - Get invoice statistics
GET /api/invoices/{id} - Get a specific invoice
PUT /api/invoices/{id} - Update an invoice
POST /api/invoices/{id}/payments - Add a payment to an invoice
POST /api/invoices/{id}/cancel - Cancel an invoice
POST /api/invoices/{id}/refund - Process a refund for an invoice
